Our Hypothesis
We believe architect engagement during the proactive motion may correlate with improved customer success scores — but we cannot yet claim it caused them. What we can say: scores moved in these accounts during the same period architects were engaged. This analysis is a starting point to understand which accounts responded, which didn't, and what questions we should be asking next. No causal conclusions should be drawn from this data alone.
