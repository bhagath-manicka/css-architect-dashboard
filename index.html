How This Started
Our architects have been engaged in the proactive motion — design reviews, security audits, go-live checks, technical debt sessions. What we weren't doing was watching what happened to the scores on these accounts. We went back and looked at 15 accounts. Some numbers moved up. Some moved down. Some held. We are not claiming architect engagement caused any of this. We genuinely don't know yet. What we're asking is: can we see a signal? And if there is one, how do we build on it? Going forward, we will spend real time looking at these numbers, understanding the needs behind them, and delivering based on what they tell us.
