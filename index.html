Why We Are Looking at This Now
We have been running the proactive motion — design reviews, security sessions, go-live checks — without these scores in the room. No decisions were being made against them. No discussions were framed around them. That changes now. We are not saying architects move these numbers. We are not claiming a direct lever. What we are saying is: these scores tell us something about the account — their technical health, their adoption maturity, their expertise gaps. When an architect walks into a conversation, they should walk in with this context. The goal is to give account owners a sharper, more informed partner — one who sees the same numbers they do.
